More than the work.

I build software, but the habits behind it come from learning, training, climbing, and always having something playing in the background.

This is the side of me that does not fit neatly inside a résumé.

02 / TRAINING

Consistency outside the screen.

Training gives me structure. It is where I practice showing up, staying patient with progress, and doing the work even when motivation is not the main thing carrying me.

03 / ROCK CLIMBING

Problem-solving with no shortcut.

Climbing is a different kind of puzzle: read the route, commit to a move, miss sometimes, adjust, and try again. That process feels familiar when I am building or debugging something difficult.

04 / HOW I THINK

The principles I keep close.

  1. Learn systems, not just steps.
  2. Build things that are useful.
  3. Get better through feedback and iteration.
  4. Stay curious long enough to finish what I start.

The work is professional. The person behind it is always growing.
